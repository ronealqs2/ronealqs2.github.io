USER-DEFINED TRAINING RUBRIC

A score supports judgment. It does not replace it.

Likelihood and impact each use a 1–5 scale; inherent score = likelihood × impact. The same bands apply to residual risk only when both residual inputs are supported.

Low1–5
Moderate6–11
High12–19
Critical20–25

The latest supplied workbook changes Low from 1–4 to 1–5 and Moderate from 5–11 to 6–11. High remains 12–19 and Critical remains 20–25. This is a project-specific rubric, not a claimed standard-mandated scale. The downloadable workbook preserves the supplied content; identifying file metadata has been removed. Operating effectiveness

PROPOSED NEXT STEPS

From scenario to a supported conclusion.

  1. Confirm scope

    Agree on systems, accounts, exceptions, assessment period and accountable participants.

  2. Obtain evidence

    Collect dated policy, enforcement configurations, exceptions and authentication records.

  3. Perform & review tests

    Document populations, samples, results and reviewer rationale. Do not infer operating effectiveness from configuration alone.

  4. Reassess & verify

    Approve treatment and dates, assess supported residual risk, and verify any closure through reviewed evidence.
