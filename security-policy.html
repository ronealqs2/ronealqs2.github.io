Security Policy Design

Designed access-control, data-protection and remote-access policy recommendations for a hypothetical defense contractor.

Requirements and Method

I developed a four-part policy report for the hypothetical Blue Stripe Tech defense-contractor scenario, researching requirements and proposing access-control, data-protection, infrastructure and remote-access policies.

Policy and Evidence Example

The table below is a new portfolio illustration derived from the project themes, not a quotation from the original report.

Illustrative requirements-to-evidence workflow
Policy areaProposed requirementEvidence to request
Access controlApprove access according to job responsibilities and review continued need.Access requests, approval records, role assignments and periodic review decisions.
Remote accessDefine authorized remote-access methods and responsibilities.Approved policy, relevant configuration records and dated access logs.
Data protectionIdentify data handling requirements and communicate protection responsibilities.Data inventory, classification rules and applicable handling procedures.

Practical Takeaway

Applicability depends on actual contracts, system ownership and data. A written policy needs separate implementation and operating evidence before effectiveness can be assessed.
